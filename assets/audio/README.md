Soft Bell, Double Chime, and Bright Tone are short signals synthesized specifically
for Jikanda from sine waves. No third-party recordings, samples, songs, or sound
libraries are used. The editable source is `scripts/generate-alarms.py`; regenerate
with `python scripts/generate-alarms.py`. Format: 44100 Hz, mono, signed 16-bit PCM.
Duration is at most 1.15 seconds. There is no looping or background audio engine.
